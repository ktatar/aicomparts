## Bio

Karthikeyan Natarajan is a Master’s student in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Chalmers University of Technology, currently conducting his thesis at Volvo Group (Volvo Trucks Technology). His research focuses on AI-driven sound diagnostics for heavy-duty vehicles, using machine learning and signal processing to detect and interpret mechanical faults. He is interested in building reliable, interpretable AI systems that connect data, engineering, and real-world impact.
