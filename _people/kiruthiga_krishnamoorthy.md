## Bio

>“Learning never exhausts the mind,  it expands the possibilities of what technology can achieve.”

Currently, she works on AI driven acoustic fault diagnosis at Volvo Trucks, developing deep learning solutions for sound analysis, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance in complex engineering systems.
